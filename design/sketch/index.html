aHoy

Design sketches

Static sketches of each aHoy screen at 1440 × 900, drawn as one figure family from DESIGN.md. The data is synthetic: a fake repo called pocketwiki. None of this ships.

  1. 1Workspace, wheel livemain.htmlThe session chart, the reading panel and the steering wheel. Hold any waypoint for 300 ms, drag toward a direction and release; Esc or the centre cancels. With the keyboard, focus a waypoint and press Space, then arrows and Enter.
  2. 2First run, new sessionstart.htmlAn empty chart with the repo picker beside it: choose a repo and a commit, and optionally ask the first question.
  3. 3Blocked command, allowed onceblocked.htmlThe badge on the waypoint, the wheel's Allow blocked… wedge, and the child step that runs the exact command once.
  4. 4Error waypoint selectederror.htmlA step that failed after its retry, with the panel showing both rejected answers, what was kept and what to do next.
  5. 5Settingssettings.htmlProvider and model, the three command modes explained, and the Safe-mode allowlist with the flags that stay blocked.

Shared pieces: sketch.css holds every token and component; chart.js draws the chart and the wheel for screens 1, 3 and 4.
